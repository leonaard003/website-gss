About Us

After years of being trusted and operating under another company's name, we are proud to introduce Gala Sukses Samudera.

This independent journey marks our commitment to continuing the legacy of quality and trust that our partners have come to know and value.

We have also officially commenced operations at our first processing facility in Medan, Indonesia. By combining a strong global network with modern infrastructure, we are ready to serve international markets with renewed passion, dedication, and excellence in service.
